Helpful answers

Before you begin.

How do I know which treatment is right for my skin?

It depends on your skin type, diagnosis, medical history, goals and examination. A qualified clinician should assess these before recommending a procedure.

Is laser treatment suitable for every skin tone?

No. The device, wavelength and settings must be selected carefully, particularly for darker skin tones, to reduce burns and pigment changes. A test spot may be recommended.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies by concern, treatment type, skin response and device. A clinician can explain the expected course and limitations during consultation.

Is there downtime after a procedure?

Some treatments cause temporary redness, swelling or sensitivity. Stronger lasers and peels may need more recovery and strict sun protection.

Are glutathione injections safe for skin lightening?

Injectable glutathione is not an established, reliably safe or proven treatment for cosmetic skin lightening. Potential risks and regulatory concerns should be discussed with a qualified physician. This site does not recommend IV glutathione for skin whitening.

Can tattoos be removed completely?

Laser removal often takes multiple sessions. Clearance varies with ink color, depth, skin tone and tattoo age; complete removal cannot be guaranteed.
